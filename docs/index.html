About

An animated show jumping arena for the CYD 4.0", the Freenove 4.0" and 2.8" and the CYD 2.8": ten riders and horses take turns over a course of five coloured fences, one with a water tray, in front of a packed grandstand. Every round is timed and a knocked rail costs 4 faults. Now and then a horse refuses: he stops, the rider turns him away and loops back to try again, and two refusals means elimination. The crowd claps at every finish and jumps up and down for a clear round. Every few rounds a little tractor rakes the ring while a sponsor board goes up over the stands. Tap the screen to ride yourself — each tap makes the horse take off, so time it before every fence. Hoofbeats, the start bell, knocked poles, applause and the tractor play through the speaker.

Version v1.6.9. WiFi is set up on the screen the first time it starts (pick your network, type the password). After that, open http://showjump40.local (Freenove 4.0": http://showjump.local, Freenove 2.8": http://showjump28.local, CYD 2.8": http://cydshowjump.local) for the leaderboard, sound and volume, flipping the screen, swap red/blue and invert colours, a screenshot button and firmware updates. BOOT shows the IP and status; BOOT during WiFi setup runs the arena offline. On the CYD boards the first start asks you to touch five crosshairs to calibrate the touch screen. Freenove boards: hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT before connecting, and press RESET after flashing.

Updating without losing WiFi: download Show-Jumping-<your board>-update.bin from the latest release, open the board's web page, and use Firmware update.

Source and build notes: Show-Jumping.

1

Firmware

This replaces whatever was on the board before. Pick the one that matches your board; the two CYD 2.8" builds differ only in the display driver. This installer wipes the board, including its saved WiFi. To update a board that is already set up, use the update file instead (see below) — it keeps WiFi and settings.

Colours wrong (negative, or red and blue swapped)? On a CYD 2.8" you most likely picked the wrong CYD build — try the other one, or use the swap red/blue and invert colours switches on the board's web page. Nothing is damaged.

2

Connect your board

Plug the device into a USB port, then click connect and pick it from the list. This requires the board to be in bootloader mode (it's what actually flashes it) — to just read logs from a board that's already running normally, use View Device Logs below instead, no bootloader mode needed.
